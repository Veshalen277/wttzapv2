# Your inventory: everyday guide

## What to look at first

- **Items you stock:** the number of active items in your inventory.
- **Running low:** items with some stock left that have reached their ordering level.
- **Out of stock:** items with nothing left.

These totals cover your whole active inventory. Searching the list does not change them. Archived items are excluded.

## Update an item

Find the item in the stock list and choose **Update stock**. Then select what happened:

- **New stock arrived:** enter the amount you received.
- **Stock was used or sold:** enter the amount that went out.
- **I counted what is left:** enter the total you actually counted, not the difference.

Add a short note so your team understands the change, then save. For example, if the system says 12 and you counted 9, choose the count option and enter 9.

## Keep item details useful

Use **Edit details** to update its name, category, supplier, price or ordering level. To add a new item, create its details first, then record the stock that arrived.

## See what happened

**Recent stock activity** shows your team's updates, quantities, notes and names. If no updates have been recorded yet, it says so. Starting balances are kept in the expandable **Record details and starting balances** section for anyone investigating an issue.

## Update several items

Open **Update several items from a spreadsheet**. The file-format section explains the columns and provides item IDs. Upload the CSV, check the preview, then apply it. If an item fails validation, none of that import is saved.

## If something looks wrong

Refresh the page first. Check recent activity in case someone else updated the item. If the balance still looks wrong, tell your supervisor which item is affected and what you expected. Do not submit repeated guesses to make a number match.

For installation, permissions and database troubleshooting, your developer can use README.md in this folder and the main dashboard README.
